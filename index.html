ABOUT THE APP

A little less scrolling. A little more discovering.

A Page a Day brings together concise educational storytelling, reflective prompts, quizzes and optional recommendations for further reading.

The app uses original summaries and commentary. It is designed to introduce ideas and books, not replace the original works themselves.

A Page a Day is free to use and supported by advertising. Ads are shown at natural breaks rather than inside the reading experience.

You do not have to read everything. Just keep discovering.
