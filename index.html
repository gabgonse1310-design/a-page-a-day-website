ABOUT A PAGE A DAY

A little less scrolling.
A little more discovering.

A Page a Day combines original summaries and commentary, reflective prompts, quizzes and optional recommendations for further learning.

It is designed to introduce books, traditions and ideas — not replace the original works themselves.

The app is free to use and supported by advertising. Ads appear at natural breaks after a completed page, never inside the reading itself.
